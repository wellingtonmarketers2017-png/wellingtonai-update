The report's proposed ten-year split is ₹45,000 crore fiscal receipts, ₹25,000 crore government savings, and ₹1,28,000 crore enterprise productivity/other economic benefits. Each line is allocated proportionally here for scenario planning. The latter can overlap with GVA and must not be added to GSDP. Annual ledger figures follow the source's rounded schedule, with the last year reconciled to the ₹1,98,000 crore total.

Investment and validation milestones

2027–2028 · Establish

Charters, baseline/counterfactuals, department pilots, project permissions, and first financed capacity. Confirm demand and unit economics before asset commitments.

2029 · Validate scale

Test the six project anchors and the ₹27,000 crore Year-3 programme envelope; distinguish ₹13,000 crore TN IGS improvement from enterprise turnover.

2030–2032 · Reinvest and expand

Scale demonstrated demand, EOU and retail supply chains, regional tourism and aviation. Validate local value-added, operating surplus and project-by-project additionality.

2033–2036 · Evidence-led expansion

Approve new capacity only against confirmed orders, environmental clearance and financing. Independently audit economic and fiscal benefits and overlap.
